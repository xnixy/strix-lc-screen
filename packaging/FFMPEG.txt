This download includes ffmpeg, a separate program that reads the photo or video before it is sent to the pump.

ffmpeg is licensed under the GNU LGPLv2.1. strix-lc-screen is MIT. They stay separate programs; this one runs ffmpeg, it is not linked into it.

The build used here is the newest ffmpeg 8.1 LGPL build from BtbN when this release was made. Run "ffmpeg -version" to see its exact version.
https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/releases/tag/latest
